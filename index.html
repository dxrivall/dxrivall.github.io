About

Derek Charland

dxrival is Derek Charland, a 24-year-old independent alternative artist from Syracuse, New York. He entered the music scene in 2019 with “High School Hate,” then stepped away to refine his sound before launching “Money To The Grave” and “Ragin’” in 2024.

His sound is an “alternative mix” that refuses one genre: metal icons Metallica, Korn, and System of a Down meet modern rage innovators Yeat and Ken Carson. He records, mixes, and masters his own work — and owns his rights and masters outright.

2025 was his biggest year yet, with no new music released: streams climbed 331% year over year, pushing past one million total streams across 134 countries.
